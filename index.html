I'm currently pursuing my PhD at Mercedes-Benz Tech Innovation GmbH in collaboration with the Institute of Computer Science at Leipzig University.

My research is focused on novel approaches and applications for formal verification techniques in the context of cybersecurity and security protocol verification in general, with an emphasis on automotive security.

The topics of my most recent publications are process mining for security protocol verification results, extracting attack resilience hyperproperties from formal verification results, and a literature survey on formal verification techniques for automotive security.
